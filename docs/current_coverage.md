# Experimental coverage

The shared `experiment_index.json` maps 22 evidence groups to 20 model-execution entries and their offline analysis IDs. It includes the main results and reported appendix experiments. Scope is experimental reproduction, not manuscript writing, plotting or typesetting.

The evidence index lists actual files, not only expected directories. Integrity verification checks their released bytes; statistical validation is reported separately.

Completed Qwen/Gemma activation and graft discovery stages are included. Incomplete Gemma graft confirmation is excluded. Teacher-forced and smoke artifacts may occur as historical provenance but are not additional paper claims.
