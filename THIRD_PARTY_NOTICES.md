# Third-party sources

Frozen exact-length examples retain their original source identifiers from Dolly, No Robots, OpenAssistant (OASST1) and WritingPrompts. Structured constraints follow COLLIE and retain CC-News, Gutenberg and Wikipedia source identifiers. These texts and upstream datasets are not relicensed by this package. Their provenance fields are retained in the frozen inputs.

The COLLIE software notice is included in `notices/COLLIE_LICENSE.txt`. NLTK's software notice is included in `notices/NLTK_LICENSE.txt`; the bundled Punkt resources retain their original README and contributor information in the reproduction package. Upstream author names in these notices are attribution, not submission-author metadata.

Model weights, Stanza model resources and the NLI model are not bundled. Obtain them from their original providers under their applicable terms. Model IDs and available frozen revisions are recorded in the reproduction configuration and evidence runtime records.
